Why

Every wallet you own hangs on one key. Reused for everything. Forever.

The same elliptic-curve key signs your swaps, your approvals, your logins and your withdrawals. One leak, one bad signature prompt, one break in the math, and all of it goes at once. A bunker takes that key out of the loop.

The lock

Hash-based keys, one per move

A bunker answers to one-time keys derived from 24 words. Each key signs exactly one order, cancel or withdrawal, then it is gone and the next one takes over. No key is ever reused.

The math

SHA-256. Nothing else.

No elliptic curve, no discrete logarithm, no lattice. Breaking a bunker's signature means inverting SHA-256: the assumption every blockchain already rests on.

The wallet

Your wallet only pays fees

Connect Phantom or create a wallet in the browser. It pays network fees and sends collateral in. It cannot trade and it cannot withdraw: that authority belongs to the words alone.

How it works

Four steps in. Every move after that, signed once.

  1. 01

    Choose a wallet

    Phantom, or a wallet created in the browser on the spot. It pays network fees and nothing more.

  2. 02

    Write down 24 words

    Generated in your browser, never sent anywhere. They derive every key the bunker will ever use, and the bunker's address is computed from them.

  3. 03

    Create and fund the bunker

    One transaction opens the bunker and its trading account. Send USDT in from your wallet: deposits never need a signature, money can always come in.

  4. 04

    Trade

    Pick a market, set size and side, sign with the next key. Orders are matched on a live Solana perp venue with up to 20x leverage.
