A trading wallet with no private key. A bunker signs every order, cancel and withdrawal with a one-time key from your 24 words. The chain verifies the key and burns it. The elliptic-curve key that signs your swaps, approvals and logins never touches the money.

SHA-256 and nothing else

How it works

A wallet, 24 words, one transaction. From then on every order, cancel and withdrawal is one signed move, and the words keep the keys.

Read the docs →
  1. 01

    Choose a wallet

    Phantom, or one made in the browser. It pays fees and sends collateral in. It has no power over the bunker.

  2. 02

    Write down 24 words

    They derive one-time keys. Each key signs once and names the next. Keep them: they are the bunker.

  3. 03

    Create and fund the bunker

    One transaction opens the bunker and its trading account. Deposit USDT, or SOL swapped on the way in.

  4. 04

    Trade, or let an agent trade

    Margin times leverage, up to 20x. Every move is one signed key. Hire Ash, or build your own.
