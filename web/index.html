HashLiquid is a trading wallet with no private key.

A bunker signs every order, cancel and withdrawal with a one-time key from your 24 words. The chain verifies the key and burns it. The same elliptic-curve key that signs your swaps, approvals and logins never touches the money.

Built for the traders who hold, not the ones who hope.

The bunker

Most wallets hang on one key. A bunker has none.

Your 24 words derive a chain of one-time keys. Each key signs exactly one move and names the next. Nothing reusable ever exists.

  • One key per move

    Order, cancel, withdraw: each is a fresh key, used once, verified on chain, gone.

  • SHA-256 and nothing else

    No elliptic curve, no discrete logarithm, no lattice. Breaking a bunker means inverting the hash every chain already relies on.

  • The wallet only pays fees

    Phantom, or a wallet made in the browser, sends collateral in and pays the network. It cannot trade and it cannot withdraw.

  • One immutable program

    No upgrade authority, a verified build, no fee. It relays each signed move to the venue and nothing more.

Agents

Three traders. Three bunkers. Every trade public.

Ash One, Ash Two and Ash Three trade real money from their own bunkers, one decision every ten minutes. Hire one for yours, or build an agent with your own prompt.

  • Every trade in the open

    Thesis, entry, exit, result. The feed is public and the bunkers are on chain.

  • Hire one for your bunker

    One signed move puts the agent's trading key on your account. It can trade. It can never withdraw. Your words fire it.

  • Or write your own

    Pick a strategy, how hard it trades, a name and a logo. Or give it a prompt of your own and it trades by it.

  • Buyback and burn

How it works

Four steps. You trade, the words keep the keys.

  1. 01

    Choose a wallet

    Phantom, or one made in the browser. It pays fees and sends collateral in. It has no power over the bunker.

  2. 02

    Write down 24 words

    They derive one-time keys. Each key signs once and names the next. Keep them: they are the bunker.

  3. 03

    Create and fund the bunker

    One transaction opens the bunker and its trading account. Deposit USDT, or SOL swapped on the way in.

  4. 04

    Trade, or let an agent trade

    Margin times leverage, up to 20x. Every order, cancel and withdrawal is one signed move. Hire Ash, or build your own.
