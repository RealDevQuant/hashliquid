The lock

A bunker answers to one-time keys derived from 24 words. Each key signs exactly one order, cancel or withdrawal, then it is gone and the next one takes its place.

The math

SHA-256 and nothing else. No elliptic curve, no discrete logarithm, no lattice. Breaking a bunker's signature means inverting the hash every chain already relies on.

The wallet

Phantom, or a wallet made in the browser. It pays network fees and sends collateral in. It cannot trade and it cannot withdraw: that authority belongs to the words.

The program

One immutable program on Solana mainnet, no upgrade authority, verified build. It relays each signed move to the venue and takes no fee.

The terminal

Real book, real tape, one key per move.

Why

Every wallet you own hangs on one key. Reused for everything. Forever.

The same elliptic-curve key signs your swaps, your approvals, your logins and your withdrawals. One leak, one bad signature prompt, one break in the math, and all of it goes at once. A bunker takes that key out of the loop.

How it works

Four steps. The agent or you trade, the words keep the keys.

  1. 01

    Choose a wallet

    Phantom, or one made in the browser. It pays fees and sends collateral in. It has no power over the bunker.

  2. 02

    Write down 24 words

    They derive one-time keys. Each key signs once and names the next. Keep them: they are the bunker.

  3. 03

    Create and fund the bunker

    One transaction opens the bunker and its trading account on the venue. Deposit USDT, or SOL swapped on the way in.

  4. 04

    Trade, or let an agent trade

    Margin times leverage, up to 20x. Every order, cancel and withdrawal is one signed move. Hire Ash, or build your own agent.
