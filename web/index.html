#1The lock

A bunker answers to one-time keys.

Your 24 words derive a chain of keys. Each key signs exactly one order, cancel or withdrawal, then it is gone and the next one takes its place. Nothing reusable ever exists.

  • Order, cancel, withdraw · one key each
  • Verified on chain · burned on use
  • The next key is named by the last · a chain, not a secret

#2The math

SHA-256 and nothing else.

No elliptic curve, no discrete logarithm, no lattice. Breaking a bunker's signature means inverting the hash every chain already relies on.

  • 840-byte one-time signatures · verified by the program
  • The address is the hash of the first key · nobody can open yours

#3The wallet

The wallet pays fees. Only fees.

Phantom, or a wallet made in the browser, sends collateral in and pays the network. It cannot trade and it cannot withdraw: that authority belongs to the words.

  • Deposit USDT, or SOL swapped on the way in
  • If the wallet's key leaks · nothing happens to the bunker

#4The program

One program. No upgrade authority. Verified.

The bunker's logic lives in one Solana program nobody can change, with a build anyone can reproduce from the public source. It relays each signed move to the venue and takes no fee.
